Our Commitment

Support that puts people before cost

Community support is part of our commitment to making therapy accessible to everyone. Through our CIC model, we provide low-cost and free sessions funded by our wider work.

This means you can access support in a safe, respectful space — without financial pressure.

🤝
No one is turned away due to inability to pay.

Who This Is For

This may be right for you if:

  • You need support but cannot afford private therapy
  • You are looking for a lower-cost option
  • You feel more comfortable in a shared or community setting
  • You want to take a first step without financial commitment
You are welcome here — exactly as you are.

What Support Includes

Community sessions provide support for:

  • Anxiety, stress, and overwhelm
  • Trauma and emotional distress
  • Grief and life challenges
  • Feeling isolated or unsupported
Sessions are delivered in a structured, safe environment guided by the AINT Model.
Community Sessions

Two community groups — clear pricing

We offer two community session groups so support stays accessible. Tell us your situation and we’ll place you in the right group.

🕊️

Group 1 — Funded Programme

Free

Free for participants. Funded through local partnerships, collaborations, and community wellbeing initiatives.

No one is turned away because they cannot pay.

💷

Group 2 — Community AINT Session

£20 first session
£10 per session thereafter

Available for any type of emotional or mental-health support. To qualify for Group 2, clients must meet one of the following:

  • Have no job
  • Have a part-time job
  • Be receiving benefits
  • Be unable to afford private therapy
  • Be unable to wait for long NHS waiting lists
⚠️

Important Note

Community support may have limited availability, structured session times, or group-based formats. If you need immediate or fully personalised support, standard private therapy may be more suitable.

The Process

Simple and supportive from the start

We aim to make the process as simple and accessible as possible.

1

Submit Request

Submit a request for support online or via phone. You do not need a referral.

2

Review

We review your situation and availability carefully to ensure you get the right care.

3

Match

You are matched to the most suitable group or session type for your current needs.

4

Details

You receive full details, times, and clear session guidance so you know exactly what to expect.

5

Begin

Begin support in a safe, non-judgemental space guided by our trained practitioners.

Community Interest Company

Why we offer community support

As a Community Interest Company, we reinvest private therapy income into providing accessible support for individuals, families, and communities.

This allows us to:

  • Reduce financial barriers
  • Support vulnerable individuals
  • Create safer, more connected communities
