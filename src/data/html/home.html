AINT Foundation CIC provides rapid, trauma-informed support for adults in distress, with a strong focus on suicide and crisis prevention. We specialise in men’s mental health, survivors of domestic and sexual abuse, and adults living with childhood trauma. Our early-help model offers stabilisation and emotional regulation within 48 hours, ensuring vulnerable individuals receive timely, compassionate support.

Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a GP referral?

No. You can contact us directly and start support without any referral.

How much does therapy cost?

Private AINT Session from £50 (60 minutes). Couples £75. Crisis-Prevention £75 (90 minutes). Community Session Group 1 is free (funded); Group 2 is £20 first session, then £10. Training from £115. Annual Membership Renewal £70.

What if I cannot afford private therapy?

We provide low-cost and free community support. No one is turned away due to inability to pay.

Are sessions confidential?

Yes. Everything you share is treated with strict confidentiality in a safe and respectful environment.

Are sessions online or in-person?

All sessions are currently delivered online through secure video, so you can access support from anywhere.

How quickly can I get an appointment?

Private sessions are usually available quickly. Urgent (SOS) sessions can often be arranged the same day or next available slot.

How do I know which option is right for me?

If you need immediate, personalised support, choose private therapy. If you are looking for low-cost access, community support may be suitable. You can also contact us and we will guide you.

What happens after I book?

You will receive confirmation and details for your session. We will guide you through the process and match you with the right support.

Do you offer urgent support?

Yes. We offer SOS sessions for urgent situations, subject to availability. If your situation is life-threatening, please contact emergency services.

What is the AINT Model?

The AINT Model is a non-judgemental, trauma-responsive approach that focuses on acceptance, trust, and safe human connection in every interaction.
