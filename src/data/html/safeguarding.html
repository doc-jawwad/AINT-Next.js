Safeguarding

A clear overview of how AINT Foundation CIC approaches safeguarding, confidentiality, and safe therapeutic practice.

Safe. Respectful. Trauma-responsive.

We want every person who reaches out to feel protected, heard, and supported within clear safeguarding boundaries.

Our Commitment

At AINT Foundation CIC, safeguarding is central to everything we do.

We are committed to providing safe, respectful, and trauma-responsive support where every individual feels protected, heard, and treated with dignity.

What Safeguarding Means

Safeguarding means protecting individuals from harm, abuse, neglect, or exploitation.

Our approach ensures that all interactions take place in a safe, supportive, and non-judgemental environment.

Our Approach

Our work is guided by the AINT Model - Acceptance, Integration, Non-judgement, and Trust.

We create spaces where individuals feel emotionally safe, without pressure or fear, while maintaining clear professional and ethical boundaries.

Confidentiality & Safeguarding

We maintain strict confidentiality across all services.

However, there are situations where confidentiality may need to be limited.

This includes when:

  • There is a risk of harm to you
  • There is a risk of harm to others
  • There are safeguarding concerns involving vulnerable individuals
  • Disclosure is required by law

In such cases, appropriate action will be taken to protect safety and well-being.

How We Respond to Safeguarding Concerns

If a safeguarding concern arises, we will:

  • Assess the level of risk
  • Take appropriate action to ensure safety
  • Involve relevant services or authorities where necessary

Our priority is always to act in a way that protects and supports individuals.

Community Responsibility

Safeguarding is a shared responsibility.

If you are concerned about your own safety or the safety of someone else, we encourage you to seek appropriate support and take action where needed.

Boundaries of Our Service

AINT Foundation CIC provides therapeutic and emotional support services.

We do not provide emergency or crisis intervention services.

Emergency Support

If you are in immediate danger or need urgent help:

  • Call 999
  • Contact NHS 111
  • Samaritans: 116 123 (24/7)

Support is available; you are not alone.

Our Responsibility as a CIC

As a Community Interest Company, we are committed to acting responsibly, ethically, and in the best interests of the people and communities we serve.
