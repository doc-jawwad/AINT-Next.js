Awards

Recognition for the AINT Model and AINT Foundation CIC in 2026.

2026 Business Elite Award — Best Trauma-Informed Community Therapy Service

AINT Foundation CIC provides rapid, trauma-informed support for adults in distress, with a strong focus on suicide and crisis prevention. We specialise in men’s mental health, survivors of domestic and sexual abuse, and adults living with childhood trauma. Our early-help model offers stabilisation and emotional regulation within 48 hours, ensuring vulnerable individuals receive timely, compassionate support.
