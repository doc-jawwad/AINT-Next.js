Why Choose Private Therapy

Private therapy is ideal if you are looking for dedicated attention without the wait. Every session is guided by the AINT Model — creating a safe, structured, and non-judgemental space from the very first moment.

This approach ensures you are supported exactly where you are, helping you build resilience and emotional clarity at a pace that feels right for you.

How Pricing Works

Private therapy pricing depends on:

  • The type of session
  • Practitioner availability
  • Whether the session is for an individual, couple, or group

This allows us to provide flexible, personalised support tailored to your needs.

Missed Session Policy

We understand that life happens. To ensure fairness and consistency:

  • No fee is charged with 24 hours’ notice
  • Sessions cancelled with less than 24 hours’ notice may be charged in full
  • Repeated last-minute cancellations may affect future bookings

This helps maintain reliability and emotional safety for all clients.
