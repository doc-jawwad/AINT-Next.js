Cookie Policy

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website.

They help websites function properly and improve user experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

AINT Foundation CIC uses cookies to:

  • Ensure the website works correctly
  • Understand how visitors use the website
  • Improve performance and user experience

We do not use cookies to identify you personally without your consent.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies (Always Active)

These cookies are required for the website to function.

They include:

  • Page navigation
  • Form submissions
  • Security features

You cannot disable these cookies.

Analytics Cookies (Optional)

These cookies help us understand how users interact with the website.

For example:

  • Pages visited
  • Time spent on site
  • General usage patterns

These cookies are only used if you give consent.

Functional Cookies (Optional)

These cookies improve your experience by remembering:

  • Preferences
  • Settings
  • Choices you make on the site

4. Third-Party Cookies

We may use trusted third-party services, such as:

  • Analytics tools (e.g., Google Analytics)
  • Booking systems (e.g., Calendly — when integrated)

These providers may place cookies to deliver their services.

All third parties are expected to comply with UK GDPR.

5. Your Consent

When you first visit our website, you will see a cookie banner.

You can:

  • Accept all cookies
  • Reject non-essential cookies
  • Manage your preferences

You can change your preferences at any time.

6. Managing Cookies

You can also control cookies through your browser settings.

You may:

  • Block cookies
  • Delete existing cookies

Note: Disabling cookies may affect how the website functions.

7. Data Protection

Any personal data collected through cookies is handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

👉 Please refer to our Privacy Policy for full details.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy to reflect changes in technology or legal requirements.

The latest version will always be available on this page.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Cookie Policy, please contact us through our website.
