Membership FAQ

What does annual membership include?

Membership renews your affiliation with AINT Foundation CIC and supports ongoing updates, practitioner resources, and the membership & licensing pathway.

Do I need training before joining?

Membership is designed to sit alongside AINT training. If you are new to the model, start with Level 1 or Level 2, then continue with annual membership.

How do I join or renew?

Use Get Membership to enquire. We will guide you through renewal and confirm the best next step for your role and training background.
