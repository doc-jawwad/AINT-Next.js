Our Story

Where It Began

AINT Foundation CIC began with a simple belief: every person deserves to be met with dignity, understanding, and compassion. That belief wasn’t born in a boardroom or a strategy meeting. It came from real people, real experiences, and years of listening to those who felt unseen in the places they turned to for help.

Through therapeutic practice, research, and lived experience, one thing became impossible to ignore. Many people were walking into traditional mental health settings and leaving feeling judged, pressured, or misunderstood. They weren’t being met where they were. They were being asked to fit into systems that didn’t understand their story.

The AINT Model was created as a response to that reality. It grew from professional insight, but also from human experience — from sitting with people in their hardest moments and hearing what they truly needed. A space where they could speak openly. A space without fear or pressure. A space where nothing about them was treated as “wrong.”

What started as a commitment to humane, accessible care has grown into a movement that now reaches individuals, families, and practitioners across the UK. Today, AINT Foundation CIC continues to offer rapid, trauma-informed support that is grounded in compassion, honesty, and real understanding.

We remain dedicated to creating spaces where people feel safe, respected, and genuinely heard. Because healing begins the moment someone feels seen for who they are.

Our Signature Method

The AINT Neuro-Regulation Method

The AINT Neuro-Regulation Method is the signature grounding technique of AINT Foundation CIC and a defining feature of the AINT Model.

It was created to solve one of the biggest challenges people face when seeking emotional support: talking about traumatic or distressing experiences without being pulled back into the pain.

The AINT Neuro-Regulation Method offers a gentle, humane alternative.

How it works

  1. You briefly name the issue that brought you to us, just a few seconds.
  2. Before your brain has time to reconnect with the emotional pain, we gently pause you.
  3. We guide you toward something positive, comforting, or light.
  4. This shift activates the brain’s natural calming chemistry.
If you cannot think of anything positive, there is no pressure. We introduce something light or neutral to help your body settle.

Why is it effective

This method works because the brain does not have time to fully activate the traumatic memory network.

It helps prevent:

PanicDissociationEmotional overwhelmShutdown

And supports:

GroundingEmotional stabilityA sense of controlSafetyConnection

This method reflects our core belief: emotional support should never retraumatise, it should stabilise and empower.

It is one of the key reasons the AINT Model has received global recognition for innovation, accessibility, and humanity.

Accessible & Structured

  • Community Session Group 2 — Community AINT Session: £20 first session, £10 per session thereafter
Group 2 eligibility — meet one of the following:
  • Have no job
  • Have a part-time job
  • Be receiving benefits
